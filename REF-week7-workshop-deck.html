Your screen works — but it talks straight to files on one laptop. New words & ideas

Five words, plainly

WordWhat it really means
APIA menu of questions your data answers over the network.
EndpointOne item on that menu. /tickets?team=NAME is an endpoint with a filter.
JSONThe answer's format — data dressed for machines, readable by humans.
TriggerCode that runs on a clock or an event, not on a click. It notices things nobody asked about.
AI use caseOne sentence: "A user asks the AI ___, and it answers ___ using ___ data."

I do · step 3 of 5

I say: "That's JSON — data dressed for machines, still readable by us. Same lie-detector counts as week 6, but now anything can ask for them: my screen does (open localhost:8000 — same page, new plumbing), a teammate's laptop could, and next week the AI helper will."

I do · step 4 of 5

I filter — then I break it on purpose

I OPEN: http://localhost:8000/tickets?team=TCS-ServiceDesk-L1-NA-WE THE SCREEN SAYS: 25 tickets, that team only — number, state, description. Then, ON PURPOSE, I leave the team off: http://localhost:8000/tickets THE SCREEN SAYS: {"error": "Say which team: /tickets?team=NAME"}

I say: "I break it on purpose, every time. A good API explains itself when you ask it badly — and this error message is one your team will rewrite for your users. Errors are part of the product."

I do · step 5 of 5 — back to that ALERT

The reflex, explained

I OPEN: http://localhost:8000/alerts THE SCREEN SAYS: [{"team": "TCS-ServiceDesk-L1-NA-WE", "open": 52, "rule": "more than 40 open incidents", "at": "10:47:12"}]

I say: "Once a minute, all by itself, the server re-checks a rule: any team over 40 open incidents. The service desk carries 52, so it fired within seconds. That's a trigger — the client hears about trouble without asking. Your team will replace my rule with one your client actually fears."
